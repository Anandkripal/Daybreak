# Data dictionary and governance

| Dataset | Grain and class | Measures and interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| cashflow | Fictional budget × month × category; synthetic | `cents`: non-negative nominal AUD cents. `kind`: income/expense. `recurring`: explicit invented flag, not inferred. Month is first calendar day. No transfers or tax calculation. |
| monthly_finance | One fictional budget × calendar month; synthetic | Income/expense/recurring/net in integer AUD cents. Net = income − expenses. Expense ratio = expenses / income; null when income is zero. Net change compares previous available month; trailing mean includes up to three observations. Cumulative net is a flow sum, not a verified bank balance. |
| assistance | Calendar month × assistance category; synthetic | Mutually exclusive outcome counts reconcile to contacts. Received-help share = received_help / contacts, undefined at zero contacts. These are invented workflow counts, not people, provider availability or real impact. Do not interpret summed category counts as unique people. |
| rba_rates | FILRSAVR × observation month; public historical | `rate_percent_pa`: per cent per annum, so 4.5 means 4.5%, not 450%. Average outstanding variable housing rate from RBA Securitisation System. Differences are percentage points. Do not sum rates. Dates are source month-end dates. |
| quality | Named export check | Difference versus independently calculated source controls. Expected difference zero. These limited checks do not establish data fitness beyond their definitions. |

`fixtures/rba_provenance.json` records the complete-download SHA-256, URL, reviewed retrieval date, period range, series, source, units and copyright URL. `fixtures/rba_selected.csv` contains only the RBA-labelled selected observations; third-party source columns and private user records are excluded. `exports/manifest.json` hashes each generated CSV separately. A hash identifies bytes, not truth or source publication time. Original observations may have been revised after historical forecast origins. No current-rate or real-time backtest claim is supported.

Financial data attribution: Reserve Bank of Australia, F5 Indicator Lending Rates, Securitisation System. Follow [RBA copyright terms](https://www.rba.gov.au/copyright/), including financial-data and third-party restrictions. This is not blanket CC BY licensing or RBA endorsement. Source verification is a historical check, not permanent freshness. Do not add new series or redistribute third-party columns without reviewing terms.

Ownership: logical Data Engineer owns export definitions and provenance; BI Analyst owns formulas/views; QA independently checks controls and output semantics. Refresh selected rates through the validated ingestion process and review metadata, row counts, period changes and terms before replacing the frozen fixture. Regenerate exports and workbook together, inspect reconciliation, review changed hashes and commit them together. Do not hand-edit CSV controls to force reconciliation.

Privacy boundary: export code accepts no arbitrary input CSV or database connection and queries no private tables. Synthetic generation is deterministic and contains no names, transaction descriptions, addresses or account identifiers. This explicit restriction prevents accidentally publishing the application's private CSV workflow data. Tableau Public must only use these supplied synthetic/public files. Future real outcomes require consent, aggregation and a separate disclosure-risk review; replacing synthetic counts with personal rows is prohibited.
